Our Programs

Five programs. One goal: stronger communities.

1. Education for All

Scholarships, school kits, after-school tutoring, computer classes and teacher training.

2. Health & Wellbeing

Free health screenings, nutrition workshops, mental health support and community health education.

3. Culture & Heritage

Folk festivals, traditional crafts, music and dance, and cultural exchange with international partners.

4. Youth Empowerment

Leadership camps, career mentoring, entrepreneurship training and digital skills for young people aged 14-30.

5. Community Development

Clean water projects, environmental clean-ups, support for small farmers and local businesses.
